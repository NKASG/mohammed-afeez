Research profile

What I research, why, and the kind of funded study I am applying for.

My work sits where hardware, AI and real-world constraints meet. I design systems that have to run on limited power, limited compute and limited data, because that is what most of Africa has to work with.

In industry I have put a camera-based PCB defect detector on a meter production line, led firmware for fleets of payment terminals, and built monitoring for field energy loggers. In research with Axum AI, I co-authored open-source tools for healthcare workers and city planners, built for data-scarce settings and published on arXiv, medRxiv and EarthArXiv.

My MSc in Financial Engineering at WorldQuant University, finishing in December 2026, added stochastic modelling, statistics and machine learning. I want a PhD where these come together: intelligent systems, from embedded AI and robots to energy and decision-support tools, built to be reliable and affordable in low-resource settings.

  • TinyML gesture & keyword models on Nano 33 BLE

  • In-firmware fault diagnostics
03

Act

Control loops that move or switch something real.

  • Closed-loop control of a 12/24/36 V push-pull inverter
  • Automatic sun-tracking solar panel
  • Relay control for home & industrial automation
  • Biometric access & attendance hardware

Power

Energy in, managed and converted for the load.

  • Buck & boost converters on ferrite-core transformers
  • Digital power supply (MEMMCOL)
  • DC metering / AMI design
  • Solar PV design & installation

Closing the loop from 04 back to 01 inside one robot is what I want graduate study to train.
